Meet Gurudev preview

The real visitor, admin and security apps side by side, on sample data. Everything you do stays in your own browser.

  1. Visitor: log in with any number (the code shows on screen), add your name and a photo, and book.
  2. Admin: open Requests and tap Approve.
  3. Tap “Skip to next message” to receive the reminder, greeting and QR pass.
  4. Security: tap the visitor's pass and Allow entry. Tap it again to see the red alert.
